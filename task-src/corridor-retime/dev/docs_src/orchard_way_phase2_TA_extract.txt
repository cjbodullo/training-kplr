LAND AT ORCHARD WAY (PHASE 2) - TRANSPORT ASSESSMENT
Extract: sections 6 (Trip Generation), 7 (Distribution) and 9 (Junction Assessment)
Prepared for the applicant, March 2025. Planning ref 24/01187/RM.

6  TRIP GENERATION

6.1  Phase 2 comprises 260 dwellings (180 houses, 80 flats) accessed solely from
     Orchard Way, which joins the A-road at the signal controlled junction referred
     to by the highway authority as J2. Phase 1 (340 dwellings) is occupied.

6.2  Vehicle trip rates were derived from the TRICS database (v7.11), land use
     03/A Residential - Houses Privately Owned and 03/C Flats Privately Owned,
     edge of town sites in England excluding Greater London, weekday surveys,
     multi-modal, 12 sites selected.

6.3  Weekday AM peak hour (08:00-09:00) vehicle trip rates per dwelling:

                         Arrivals   Departures   Two-way
       Houses             0.141       0.428       0.569
       Flats              0.078       0.236       0.314

6.4  Resulting Phase 2 AM peak hour vehicle trips:

                         Arrivals   Departures   Two-way
       Houses (180)          25          77         102
       Flats (80)             6          19          25
       Total                 31          96         127

7  DISTRIBUTION

7.1  Distribution follows the 2011 Census journey to work data for the MSOA:

       East via A-road (right turn out of Orchard Way at J2)    70%
       West via A-road (left turn out of Orchard Way at J2)     30%

7.2  Phase 2 AM peak departures at J2: right turn +67 veh/h, left turn +29 veh/h.

9  JUNCTION ASSESSMENT

9.3  J2 was assessed in LinSig with the existing fixed time plan (110 s cycle,
     Orchard Way stage 30 s) and 2024 survey flows plus Phase 2 traffic. The
     Orchard Way right turn lane operates at a degree of saturation of 88.6%
     in the AM peak with Phase 2, within the 90% practical capacity. No
     mitigation at J2 is required.
