## instruction.md

```markdown
We run crewbook, the job and invoicing app for trade businesses, with one PostgreSQL database per customer business, forty of them on one cluster. Release 5.0 needs every one of those databases to be exactly the schema in /app/target/V5__target_schema.sql, holding data that means what /app/target/CONTRACT.md says it means, and I want to get there with one script that fleetctl runs against every tenant the way /app/target/RUNNER.md describes. Write it to /app/converge.sql.

The tenants have not looked alike for years. Flyway built all of them from the migrations in /app/repo, but after four and a half years of releases, reruns and fixes made by whoever was on call that night, no two of them are quite the same any more, and only some of what happened was ever written down. Nobody can reach the cluster from this box. What each tenant went through survives only in what ops kept under /app/fleet and /app/ops, which /app/README.md lists, and some of it is wrong.

A tenant counts as converged when, after both of the runs in RUNNER.md, its schema matches V5 object for object, every row holds what the contract says it has to hold, every table hands out the right next id and flyway_schema_history is untouched. All forty have to converge or the task scores zero. Nothing here holds a copy of any tenant's real state to check your script against.

Grading happens in a separate container after this one is gone. It restores its own sealed copies of the forty databases, runs /app/converge.sql against them exactly as RUNNER.md says and compares every tenant with a sealed copy of the expected outcome. It reads /app/converge.sql and nothing else, so changing the records under /app changes nothing that is graded.

You have 18000 seconds to complete this task. Do not cheat by using online solutions or hints specific to this task.
```
